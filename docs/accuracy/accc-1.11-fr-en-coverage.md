explicitly accounts for every numbered technical section in chapters 3–29. “Clean” means no
language difference capable of changing a repository rule, test premise, RTL interpretation,
hardware interpretation, or useful author feedback was found; it is not hardware validation.
Finding IDs refer to `accc-1.11-fr-en-differences.md`.

Technical information is sourced from *The Amstrad CPC CRTC Compendium* by Longshot
(CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

Chapters 1–2 are edition metadata, licensing, and front matter and were excluded from the
implementation sweep. Parent headings without independent normative prose inherit the status
of their listed children.
