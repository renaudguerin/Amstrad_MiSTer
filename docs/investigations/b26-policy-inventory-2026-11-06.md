# B26 model inventory at 1e95fa0

The classic type 0 engine can represent the vertical action owed after an R0 widening with one bit. Its two writers have different admission rules, but the row counter consumes the same action in both cases. The horizontal deadline for old R0=1 remains underdetermined. A paired program now makes the predicted difference observable on an original CPC. [Terminal contract, proof and physical experiment](b26-type0-r0-contract-2026-11-06.md)

All line numbers below refer to **1e95fa0a802ea0cf04bb7e06d50dff9370e204b8**. The inventory covers the classic rule engines and wrapper, the Plus video/GA paths and their production consumers. It counts **32 decisions** across the two tables (4 sourced outcomes, 17 empirical models, 7 coverage assumptions and 4 initialization policies). Each row has one primary classification. **S** denotes an outcome specified by the French ACCC or distinguished on original hardware. **M** denotes an empirical implementation for a bounded outcome. **G** denotes a coverage guard or an assumption whose extension lacks independent support. **I** denotes a power-up or snapshot policy. Flip-flop count and policy count answer different questions. A sourced outcome can have an inferred implementation, and several consumers can share one policy. The rows include relevant decisions carried over from the [first inventory](rtl-model-confidence-2026-11-06.md). This is a scoped sample of decisions, not a count of all chip quirks.

### Hardware outside the counted decisions

The classic GA provides an additional boundary check outside the two tables. Its byte-anchor replay, queued mode change and blank-to-RGB phase are implementation choices around French §9.3.4 pp56–57. The normal/JIT and blank-reactivation dot positions constrain observable RGB. Separate shifter/mux staging can generate those same dots. Do not transfer the classic serializer's internal staging to the Plus mode latch. See `rtl/GA40010/video.sv:59–89,105–134` and [classic endpoint evidence](hardware-runs/classic-type0-mode-boundary-2026-11-04.md).
