## Mechanism boundary and next proof

A focused Astra high source consultation checked French renders §4.4.4 p31,
§16.2.1 p162 and §16.4.2 p171. Separating bus capture, consumer visibility and
comparator observation is plausible, but the aperture remains unidentified.
The source's OUT(C)/OUTI black-onset distinction cannot be collapsed into one
fixed character sampling edge without explaining the measured phase elsewhere.
Manufacturer E-fall setup/hold does not prove internal transparency. Existing
B8's early-storage assertion is an implementation contract, not independent
hardware proof. R5/R0 already capture events separately from later consumers.

The [midrow onset pair](crtc1-r7-onset-controls-2026-11-04.md) is now
independently measured9/5 in AmSpirit; local replay is provisionally7/3. An
ordinary NJIT control (source6) is next to separate shared blanking from write
observation. Authentic B8 OUTI qualification remains unavailable; the original
BCTRL scanner uses OUT(C),C. Any eventual mechanism must also cover origin DI bypass,
terminal-C0/nonterminal-raster equality, held writes, false/rearm, snapshot
replacement and live-type round trips. Keep the XFAIL until these constrain a
general rule; do not fit a numerical R7-only delay to the fifth case.
