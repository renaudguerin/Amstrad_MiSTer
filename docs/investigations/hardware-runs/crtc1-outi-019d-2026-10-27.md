# CRTC 1 OUTI R7 boundary (`019D`), 2026-10-27

At base `29eff0d`, a production T80pa and Gate Array replay of the actual
`SHAKE27B` routine `8D4C` on CRTC type 1 returned PPI `1F` for both the fifth
(`HL=05EF`) and fourth (`HL=05EE`) variants. The Logon System hardware
reference gives `1E` and `1F` respectively. The fifth write of R7=32 occurs at
C4=32, C9=7, C0=63, eight master ticks before the next C0 edge; the fourth is
at C0=62. Current storage and effects occur at the first system edge after the
strobe level. Four B8 cases pass. Evidence is in
`.roster-scratch/019d/replay.log` and `.roster-scratch/019d/fail-before.log`.

The focused vector `outi_r7_boundary_vsync` in `sim/crtc_t80_test.cpp` uses
R4=38, R7=30 and R9=7, then writes R7=32 at the authentic C4/C9/C0 phase. It
checks that VSYNC is initially low and that the next C0 edge is eight master
ticks away. The fifth variant reproduces the mismatch without an RTL change;
the fourth passes. The fifth remains
an XFAIL, with XPASS fatal. No RTL fix is justified by the evidence yet.

The French ACCC v1.11 was read directly, including rendered pages. The relevant
anchors are §13.3 p.115, §13.6.2 p.124 and §13.7.1.1 p.126; the latter describes
effective R0 comparator timing, not a universal register latch. §4.4.4 p.28 and
diagram B p.31 distinguish periodic access and OUTI phases without specifying
an exact latch edge. §16.4.2 p.171 says an R7=C4 write triggers VSYNC
immediately, without defining a sub-character edge.

The [board transcription](https://github.com/pelrun/cpc-schematics/blob/master/cpc6128/mc0020x.kicad_pcb)
shows CRTC E as `~(~IOWR & ~IORD)`, separate from CCLK, matching `io_rd | io_wr`.
The [UM6845R manufacturer PDF](https://www.theoddys.com/acorn/the_6845_crtc/UM6845R-RA-RB%20UMC.pdf)
(PDF page 3, printed p.5-28) specifies write-data setup and hold at E falling,
but does not specify internal transparent visibility. A trailing-E or blanket
delay conflicts with the existing B8 OUTI R5 test, which expects its side effect
before the next CLKEN six ticks after storage. That is a simulation contract,
not independent hardware proof of early visibility; it must be re-examined
against hardware before a common latch change. An R7-only exception is not justified.
